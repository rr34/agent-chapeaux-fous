Time v3 Agent

Your work.
Connected.

Time v3 Agent is a personal AI assistant that helps you manage life and work. Type a request or speak it aloud to organize tasks, manage your calendar, find contacts, keep a journal, work with email, and receive payments. Connected services can extend it into accounting, property management, and other parts of your work.

Ask naturally, or name a role when it helps: “Time v3 Agent, as my accountant, show me this month’s transactions.” Roles are optional, and one request can draw on several connected capabilities.

Time v3 builds technology that illuminates reality. The Agent puts that mission into practice by bringing related work and evidence together: an expense can belong in accounting, a maintenance job on a task list, its appointment on a calendar, and its charge in a payment record.
